## The design iteration (SR6), on a synthetic external run

Synthetic: 6 admitted case tests and 2 generic checks blocked by safe mode, on a made-up `live` target with external reach. It is not a recorded run.

- **Before** (iteration 2's report header): `Strongest claim: Seen on the live system, stable 3/3 · strategies: reach=external, spec=served, auth=token_header, data=spec_derived, state=namespace, mutation=none, safety=safe_mode, checks=pytest_cases`
- **After** (claim first): `live · external · isolation 2 (live and local) · Seen on the live system, stable 3/3 · ran 6 of 8; blocked ▸ excluded: safe_mode 2/8 (25%) · rests on: real 6/6 (100%)`

**Why it changed:** the old line gave the ceiling and then a list of strategy names. A reader couldn't tell:
- which system it was;
- how isolated the run was (DR-3);
- how much of the plan ran, and why the rest didn't (CR4);
- whether a result rested on a real system or a stand-in (G3, SR4, DR-7).

The new line states the claim, then its limits, then its basis, in that order. The old line stays underneath in the report, for the strategy detail.
