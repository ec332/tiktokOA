## Reading it

1. **Real bug: only the platform finds it.** The naive arm passed on reference by construction (repair/drop towards green), so a test of O-6 can't survive; the SUT's tests don't probe negative `skip`/`limit`. This is the headline the design predicted.
2. **Bug-validating share: platform 5–9× higher.** 9 of its 59 tests flip on a bug/fix pair, against 1–2 per suite for the others.
3. **Seeded, authored: a tie at 2/3, different bugs.** The platform misses `INACTIVE_LOGIN` (no criterion targets it; it's caught by no platform test); every naive sample catches it and misses `TITLE_MAX`. With one author for requirements and seeds (B10), this column is weak evidence either way.
4. **Mutation strength favours the other arms, and mostly measures something else.** Of the SUT arm's 109 kills, only 34 mutants are killed by an `AssertionError`; most failures are crashes (`TypeError` 436, `ValidationError` 186, `UnmappedInstanceError` 118) because those tests build their data in the test body with `crud`, which is the mutation scope. Assertion-only kills: SUT 34, naive 48/43/43, platform 15 (gate counts ASSERTION/CONTRACT/PRODUCT_ERROR). The platform builds data in setup (mutant off, gate D10), and its own requests reach only `deps.get_current_user` (gate 002). So the scope `crud`/`deps`/`security` sits under the platform's data layer; strength here says "exercises the internals", not "checks the requirements". Widening to route handlers (K8, M7) is what would make it comparable. Assertion-only numbers: computed from `<work>/<arm>/mutants/matrix/m/*.jsonl` (`failure.phase == "call"` and error starting `AssertionError`); not in the report artifact.
5. **Race flag:** naive samples 2–3 fail 4 tests consistently in-process; the platform's x252 runs were over the network. Not comparable; outside the headline.

## How the build differs from the design

- No other deviation from B1–B10. Naive samples are separate rows (no median row): less code, shows the spread.
- The platform arm's fixed-variant verdicts come from the retest's `fail_to_pass` list only (the nodes that matter for O-6), not a full run.
