## Where the build differs from the brief or the design

1. **The dependency families are registered in their own tuple** (`families.DEPENDENCY`), not in `FAMILIES`. They are planned only when `$TP_FAMILIES` carries wired dependencies. `FAMILIES` keeps meaning "spec-derived, always planned", so `platform_rt/tests/test_rt_families.py`, which isn't mine, is unchanged and green.
2. **The Mailpit stand-in listens on port 25** (`--smtp 0.0.0.0:25`), and a read `*_PORT` name is set to `"25"`, so an app's default SMTP port works. Its `port` in the entry is 8025, the HTTP port the runner reads the journal and chaos on.
3. **Fault knobs:** the defaults are an 8 s delay and a 4 s limit. `$TP_FAMILIES` may override them (`dependency_delay_s`, `dependency_deadline_s`); the tests use 3 s and 1.5 s to stay fast.
4. **Not built:** a `setup` review item per unwired dependency. Questions are created only in `discover/stage.py`, which isn't mine. The ledger carries the reason (request R5).
5. **Discovery was tested through a stand-in for `Discovery`** (`FakeD` in the tests). The full `Discovery.run()` can't reach the fixture until P2's Dockerfile reach and stand-in provisioning land. The restart there goes through `Stack.start()` and `make_auth(...).principal()`.
